## The one sentence

> **RIG App Studio is the engine that turns a product intent into a robust,
> maintainable, scalable, *actually-deployed* full-stack application — deterministic
> where correctness must be guaranteed, probabilistic only where judgment adds value
> — so Mike can build and ship products repeatedly without the result being fragile.**

## What it IS

- A factory for a **fixed set of ~4 archetypes**, each built **as deep and
  bulletproof as a single-archetype product** (Mike: "#3 but as deep as #1 for all 4").
- **Visual, always.** Mike *sees the code* (not text), *sees the process steps*, and
  *sees the expertise and processes* built into each run.
- Output that **deploys + runs reproducibly (KPI #1)** AND **scales to production
  load (KPI #3)**. Maintainability (#2) is required but secondary in sequencing.

## What it is NOT

- Not vibe-coding (Mike: "vibe coding does not lead to a robust, solid app").
- Not a one-off demo generator · not a design tool · not a hosting platform · not no-code.

## KPIs (primary → secondary)

1. **Deploys + runs, reproducibly** — a spec produces a full-stack app that actually
   deploys and runs, byte-reproducibly on the deterministic floor.
2. **Scales to production load** — output architecture survives real traffic without rework.
3. **Maintainable by a human** — clean, conventional, documented; extensible without a fight.

## The three forks Mike handed to RESEARCH (the Q→R mandate)

Mike answered three of four intent forks with *"what does research tell us."* These
are now research questions, NOT assumptions. Step Q→R must answer them with graded,
sourced evidence:

1. **Archetypes** — what 4 archetypes are actually achievable *deep*? What full-stack
   surface is realistic, and what OSS/plug-and-play exists so we don't rebuild it?
2. **Det/prob boundary** — Mike's exact question: *"we will have to build with AI, but
   how the app runs and builds out the next one should be… what?"* Where is the line
   that yields robust (not vibe-coded) apps **and** a robust *generator*?
3. **Form factor** — what delivers "see the code + process steps + expertise"? (CLI /
   MCP / app "all suck" today — find what, done right, actually works.) Mike: visual always.

KPI is decided (#1 + #3); the bar "deploys + runs + scales reproducibly" is itself a
research target (what production-grade output requires per archetype).
